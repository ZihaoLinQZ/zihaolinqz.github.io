I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the **University of California, Davis**, advised by [Prof. Lifu Huang](https://wilburone.github.io/). My research aims to build **multimodal agents** that can perceive, reason, and act in complex real-world environments, and that **keep improving themselves** from their own experience.

My work spans multimodal agents for video, document, and 3D tasks; self-evolving agents and agent harnesses; multimodal reasoning and reasoning-driven generation; and applications of these systems in medicine and personalization. I have spent time as a research scientist intern at **Meta** and **Adobe Research**.

Before UC Davis, I received my M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from **Duke University** (2022) and my B.S. in Financial Mathematics from the **Southern University of Science and Technology** (2019).

I am always happy to collaborate. Feel free to reach out at [qzlin@ucdavis.edu](mailto:qzlin@ucdavis.edu) or [zihao.lin.0123@gmail.com](mailto:zihao.lin.0123@gmail.com).
