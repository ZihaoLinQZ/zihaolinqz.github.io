<p style="color: #d32f2f;"><strong>I am actively seeking a Summer 2027 Research Scientist Intern position, as well as full-time Research Scientist opportunities starting after my Ph.D. graduation at the end of 2027.</strong></p>

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the **University of California, Davis**, advised by [Prof. Lifu Huang](https://wilburone.github.io/). My research aims to build **multimodal agents** that perceive, reason, and act in complex real-world environments, and that **keep improving themselves** from their own experience. Toward this goal, I study how agents tackle video and document tasks, how they self-evolve through better agent harnesses, and how multimodal reasoning can drive both understanding and generation. I also apply these systems to medicine and personalization.

I have spent time as a research scientist intern at **Meta** and **Adobe Research**, and I serve as an **Area Chair** for ACL ARR.

Before UC Davis, I received my M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from **Duke University** (2022) and my B.S. in Financial Mathematics from the **Southern University of Science and Technology** (2019).

I am always happy to collaborate. Feel free to reach out at [qzlin@ucdavis.edu](mailto:qzlin@ucdavis.edu) or [zihao.lin.0123@gmail.com](mailto:zihao.lin.0123@gmail.com).
